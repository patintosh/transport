Deutsche Bahn to acquire EWS
Wednesday 07 November 2007

The European Commissions has today, Wednesday 7 November 2007, approved the proposed acquisition of English Welsh & Scottish Railway Holdings Limited (EWS) by Deutsche Bahn.

A EWS spokesperson said: ?EWS and Deutsche Bahn welcome the decision announced today by the European Commission. Both companies look forward to working together to develop rail freight in Europe. The business strategy of EWS remains the same; to continue growing rail freight services through its operations in Britain and through Euro Cargo Rail in France.?
