Communiqué de Baltrans (Hong-Kong)

BALtrans? New Office in France in Full Operation
BALtrans Holdings Limited (?BALtrans?) is pleased to announce the official opening of a new office in France. The new office at the CDG airport in Paris is now fully operational and at your service to handle your sea and air business.
Mr. Jean Claude Melin, Managing Director of BALtrans Logistics France, is a logistics veteran with 30 years hands-on experience in air and ocean business in various sizable logistics corporations.
?Focusing on international and domestic forwarding, custom clearance and warehousing, BALtrans Logistics France is expected to generate most of its business from importers in the Far East and the USA, and from key customers served by BALtrans? EMEA network,? said Mr. Melin, ?and we are confident we can strengthen the network coverage to provide customers with enhanced services?.
The new office in France marked another important step of BALtrans? expansion in Europe, Middle East and Africa (?EMEA?) region.
